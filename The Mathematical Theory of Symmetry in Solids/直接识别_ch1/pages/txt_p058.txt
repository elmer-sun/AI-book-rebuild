Seitz notation for space-group operators
A convenient notation to represent a general space-group operation is to use the
symbol {R \ v} with the meaning that R is a point-group operator and v is the trans-

acts on the points of space and that these are always referred to a fixed set of axes,
that is, it is an active operator (see section 1.4). The symbols {R \ v} were introduced
