1.5. Space groups
Before discussing the definition of a space group it is desirable to consider what is
meant by a Bravais lattice. A lattice is a collection of mathematical points arranged
in such a way that each lattice point has the same environment in the same orientation.

composed are found to be arranged in a regular manner (Haiiy 1815a-6?). That is,
