DEFINITION 1.3.7. Classification of reps
(i) A rep F is of the first kind if F is equivalent to a group of real matrices.
(ii) A rep F is of the second kind if F is equivalent to F* but not to any group of
real matrices,
(iii) A rep F is of the third kind if F is not equivalent to F*.
