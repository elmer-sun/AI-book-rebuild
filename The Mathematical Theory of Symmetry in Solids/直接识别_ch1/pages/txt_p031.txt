DEFINITION 1.3.5. Invariant subspace. Let y be a representation of G so that
T = yG is a group of non-singular linear operators acting on a vector space V. U is
said to be an invariant subspace of V under T if
(i) U is a vector subspace of V, and
(ii) TGx e U for all TG e T and for all x e U.
